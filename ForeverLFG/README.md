## Partners for group and elite quests (beta)

Open **Options → AddOns → ForeverLFG**. The introduction appears shortly after entering the world. Its first page has three short steps: activities and languages, choosing a mode, then refreshing and contacting players. The next two pages introduce quest partners and let you choose a minimap icon or compact bar. Relevant screenshot details are shown alongside each instruction, with both modes visible together. Instructions and controls use your client language. The original screenshots retain their English text, as stated in the caption. Image controls do not perform game actions. More addon users means a better chance of finding quest partners.

**Next** on the search page records that you read it. **Done** on the final page applies your launcher choice and records quest-guide completion. The updated search page is offered once to users of the previous guide without resetting quest-guide completion or the launcher. **Later**, the close button or Escape leave unfinished pages for your next login or UI reload. Combat and loading screens defer the guide. Replay all three pages with **ForeverLFG introduction** in settings or `/flfg tutorial`.

**Use a minimap icon instead of the bar** reflects your current choice; turn it off for the bar. Characters without a personal choice inherit the display selected in the guide; an existing personal choice takes priority. Position settings remain per character. Replaying or cancelling the guide does not erase completion. Settings defaults do not reset it. Quest exchange runs automatically whenever the addon is active. The old exchange checkbox has been removed; a previously saved disabled choice no longer stops sharing. ChannelBus is included; no separate download is required.

Active unfinished group and elite quest IDs, zone, level, class, client language and party status are shared. Normal selection uses the Group tag, a suggested group of at least two players, or the game's elite quest flag; explicit raid tags are excluded. Channel matches require the same selected active quest ID and known faction. Both players must be on the quest destination map returned by the receiver’s GetQuestUiMapID(questID, true), or its descendant maps, including nested caves and dungeon floors. Sharing a continent or an unrelated zone is insufficient; unknown quest maps or map ancestry hide the channel match. The sender’s existing map field is reused; quest destination maps are not transmitted. Its last received location can lag behind actual movement until the next update or expiry. Region, realm and game project remain metadata and do not restrict matching, including empty region/realm and project 0. Changes are combined; a fresh snapshot is sent about once a minute while the selected quest list is nonempty. Login or UI reload sends one initial snapshot, even if empty. Losing the last selected quest sends one empty snapshot to clear the previous card, then periodic quest messages stop. A new selected quest resumes sharing. Failed publications retry with a fresh snapshot. After three minutes without an update, the received player card expires. Quests and settings are not deleted. Self is excluded by the complete character name. Current party/raid members may also match through the channel; a valid channel result takes priority over the local result for the same player and quest. Outside the party/raid, candidates must be solo or the leader of a non-raid group with fewer than five members. Characters with the same first name and different surnames stay distinct. If your identity is not ready, snapshots and candidate matching wait until it can be read.

The separate **GroupQuests** module checks your selected active quests against connected party and raid members using `C_QuestLog.IsUnitOnQuest`. It starts after login or a roster/quest change, waits briefly for group data to load, retries during the first eight seconds and then checks every 15 seconds. The other members do not need an addon. Only a public `true` creates a match; unavailable names, identity, metadata or quest data are retried. Group results are local, kept in memory, and do not enter ChannelBus or its peer cache. These local matches do not require matching maps or a known quest map: they can appear even when a member is in another zone, if the game provides the required data. They fill gaps left by the channel and never duplicate a valid channel result. Only the addon user sees the browser; members without the addon receive no synthetic snapshot or interface. Leaving the group, disconnecting, changing identity or losing the shared active quest removes the result.

Local results use the same browser, distinct quest/player counts, new-match animation and sound settings. Regular checks do not repeat the cue, and an open browser updates quietly. **Invite** is disabled for your own group members with an explanatory tooltip; **Write** remains available and rechecks the quest before preparing a private draft. Their client language is not available from the group API, so the draft uses the existing English fallback. Party checks have a user-provided positive measurement on client 70374; the new integration and raid checks still need in-game verification.

**Left-click the minimap icon** to open or close the quest browser, or use `/flfg quests` to open it. The icon shows the number of distinct quests with current matches in a separate numeric badge, including zero. Hovering shows the full title, the number of shared quests and the number of distinct players across them. Drag with the left mouse button around the minimap; the angle is saved for your character. Dragging does not toggle the browser. The icon follows the minimap's position, scale and size, supports round/square shapes and common corner shapes when another addon provides GetMinimapShape, and does not move the minimap or its other buttons. The artwork uses a large ivory partners symbol, a gold rim and the Horde/Alliance background; an unknown faction uses a neutral base. The symbol stays bright when the count is zero. The count sits below the symbol without covering it.

The optional **Group & elite quests bar** keeps its existing count labels and saved position. You can drag it against any screen edge without it bouncing inward when released; it remains within the screen. Previously saved positions stay in place until you move the bar again. The icon and bar are alternatives; changing display keeps the current selection, unread matches and both saved positions. In combat, creating, moving or changing the display waits until after combat; an already open browser can still be closed by clicking the visible launcher. If the minimap is not ready, the bar provides a temporary entry point.

A new player/quest pair briefly lights the whole minimap medallion, or produces a brighter, wider light sweep with a warm background glow on the optional bar, even when the number of matched quests stays the same. Arrivals within two seconds share one cue. The unread mark and its tooltip remain until you open the browser; they clear when the matches disappear. Regular synchronization and short connection gaps do not announce the same pair again. An already open browser updates quietly. Before alerting, the addon rechecks your active quests, current zone and party; missing data delays the cue.

In **Options → AddOns → ForeverLFG**, **Highlight new quest matches** and **Sound for new quest matches** are enabled by default. These choices are saved. Turning off animation keeps the static unread mark. The icon glow lasts 1.8 seconds and leaves the count readable. The sound uses a subdued built-in game notification chime, with at least 30 seconds between automatic cues. **Test sound** plays the cue once; **Test animation** previews the animation on the selected icon or bar. Both work with their automatic settings disabled, without creating matches, changing the unread mark or opening a window. Testing sound does not change the automatic cue cooldown. If combat prevents creating the selected launcher, try the animation test after combat. Game sound settings remain in control. An unavailable sound API does not interrupt the visual indicator.

The browser header shows **Shared quests: X of Y - Players: Z**: X distinct shared quests, Y active quests selected by normal/debug mode, and Z distinct players across those quests. A player with several shared quests is counted once. The title and description wrap without shrinking the text; header height follows the text. Only quests with matches appear in the upper list, with level, location and player count. Click **Shared quests** to collapse or expand the quest list. Its arrow points up to collapse and down to expand; hovering explains the action. The player section is hidden too. Your quest selection is kept and restored when you expand the section, if the match is still current. Counts continue updating while collapsed; updates do not expand the list. Click a row to show its players directly underneath, before the next quest; click it again to collapse those players. Only one quest is expanded at a time: selecting another replaces the previous player list. Clicking the quest title opens your journal.

The banner’s artwork follows your character’s faction; an unknown faction uses a neutral background. This does not change matching. Player rows show a class icon, a light-colored name and compact level/class, with **Write** and **Invite** buttons. Hovering the row shows full player details and the party size for players already in a party. The Solo line is not displayed. Disabled actions are dimmed; their tooltips explain combat, an existing party, leader permissions, a full party or a raid. Client language, faction and portraits are not displayed. Player appearance cannot be retrieved from a remote name alone. Quest rows use a neutral parchment icon; class icons use the client’s class texture, with a question mark for an unknown class or missing class coordinates. A shared quest does not prove the same layer or phase. Channel discovery requires compatible addons on both players; local party and raid checks only require ForeverLFG on your character.

**Write** prepares an editable whisper in the recipient's client language with the complete link returned by the game; **Invite** is a separate click. The link is kept unchanged, including the sender's localized quest title, color and link data. The fixed **[Quest]** label has been removed after a user reported that the link looked correct in the draft but was no longer clickable after sending. If the game has no valid link, the draft uses the quest ID. A shorter template keeps long drafts within 255 bytes while preserving the complete link. The recipient is verified after chat addon hooks. If a custom editor cannot be verified, a separate draft window shows the recipient and sends only when you click **Send private message** or press Enter.

The quest list scrolls with its expanded section. Opening a row shows its player section and the following quest card in full when they fit in the viewport. Long player lists also scroll inside the parchment section; the wheel scrolls the quest list if the player list is too short to scroll, or has reached the limit in that direction. Scrolling does not change the selected quest. The window grows downward from its top left corner as quests or players are expanded. Drag the banner or resize the window; the top position is saved and restored even if the content height changes. The initial width is 400 UI units, with a minimum of 360; height follows the visible content, up to a saved limit of 560 by default. More quests or players use scrollbars. Size limits and position are saved for the character. The old 0.6.8 size is reset once while its position is kept. Data updates refresh the open browser and the selected launcher counter, including expired matches and changes to the zone or party. Click the minimap icon or optional bar to open the browser or click it again to close it. Dragging does not change the browser’s visibility. Closing preserves your current quest selection. In combat, the visible launcher continues showing counts, but opening and moving it are unavailable; clicking it can still close an already open browser. Closing the browser keeps it closed until you open it again. There are no automatic quest popups or notification controls. Old notification settings are ignored.

`/flfg dchanel` opens a movable, resizable window with **All, Quests, Trade and Matches** filters. Scroll with the mouse wheel. The last 200 logical messages are kept in memory. **Copy tab** includes every retained entry in the selected tab, including off-screen entries and full stored diagnostic fields, without the short on-screen preview limits. Matches exports the current candidate list; Trade exports the retained diagnostic summaries, not discarded chat batch payloads. The embedded reader observes currently joined system Trade, Trade Services, General and LookingForGroup channels, plus SAY and YELL, using the same ten language rule sets as ForeverChatProbe. Private, guild, party, raid, custom and addon chat are excluded. It does not automatically join extra public channels or read your professions, bags or recipe book. Recognized profession links, crafting requests/offers, item trades, services and group adverts are shared as observations, not verified player capabilities. Incoming chat batches have no feature consumer or automatic relay; the monitor keeps only their counts. No chat history enters SavedVariables.

A batch is detached atomically from the collector and held until its ChannelBus ticket finishes. New arrivals stay in the collector. Queue rejection, expiry or an explicit send failure retains the batch for retry after 10 seconds. Native `nil` results are marked unconfirmed rather than blindly resent; there is no receiver acknowledgement. While a batch is unfinished, the 45-second poll does not drain again. Batches normally contain up to 64 retained records and use a 32 KiB export budget, with the remaining tail left for later polls; a single large record stays intact. The collector is capped at 500 records, as in the diagnostic addon. During a prolonged outage or sustained overload, older buffered records can be evicted and are counted. Reloading or leaving the game clears this memory-only queue.

## Search languages

1. Open Group Finder's **magnifier** tab. Select the native category and activities above the list. If a category is hidden for your level, enable **Show activities of all levels** below the selectors.
2. Choose **Languages** in the right panel: all available languages, the game's default, or several individual languages. Your choice is saved immediately.
3. Click the single **Refresh** button above the list. Changing languages highlights it; hovering explains whether a new search is required, running, delayed, failed or unconfirmed.

Role, level, class, playstyle and display changes filter received results immediately. They do not send a search. Native searches when opening the window or changing activities keep their game behavior. Their responses do not confirm your selected languages; use Refresh to apply them. Requests are manual, with a five-second interval, and no automatic retry. Clearing all languages disables Refresh.

If the client blocks the custom search button, the original game button returns. Language selection becomes unavailable and its tooltip explains that the selected languages are unconfirmed. Other available local filters remain usable. The game keeps its restrictions, confirmations and automatic searches.

## Find a group or recruit

New characters start with **No filters**; saved modes and roles are preserved. The right panel contains **Mode → Languages → Playstyle**:

- **Find group:** select a free role to filter dungeon groups. Multiple roles match any selected role. Groups start expanded; players remain available in their own section.
- **Recruit:** players start expanded and groups collapsed. **By party composition** matches missing roles for a supported five-player dungeon party. Unknown party roles pause automatic matching. Clicking a role switches to manual selection. Level bounds and multiple classes add local conditions.
- **No filters:** shows received listings without local role, level, class or playstyle restrictions. Display preferences and the native search context remain.

Expand either section at any time. Counters show local matches out of received listings, not every listing on the server. Listings with unknown roles or filter data are kept separately. Hover **Level** or either input for help: left is the inclusive minimum, right the inclusive maximum. A blank input removes that bound; both blank remove the level filter. Enter or leaving an input applies it, and Tab switches inputs. Invalid level input stays visible for correction while the last valid range remains active. **Clear conditions** resets local conditions while preserving mode, languages, activities and display preferences.

**Write** opens private input for that group's leader without sending text. **Invite** rechecks the current player and native permissions, including assistants. Your own listing, its removal control and native right-click actions remain available. Locations can be missing or outdated; no live tracking is promised.

**Order** has separate group and player choices. Game order preserves the native order within each section; other choices use name, party size or player level. Friends/guild priority is optional. Player and group leader locations are always shown; unavailable locations are marked unknown. **Save preset** stores settings for the current character, including the chosen search languages. Loading it does not publish a listing or send a request; changed languages require Refresh. An existing preset name requires confirmation before replacement.

The activity menu adds recommended levels from game data. **Show activities of all levels** starts off. Upgrading from 0.7.0–0.7.3 turns off its old automatic setting once; later explicit manual changes are preserved. The checkbox stays visible below the selectors. Its tooltip explains the shared setting and the possible category/activity reset. The game’s own activity filter selects suitable levels and keeps activities from your active listing available. The listing form uses the same value. Changing it does not remove entry requirements or change the recruit level filter. Missing recommendations are left blank.

The combined window uses native artwork and controls. The settings column scrolls when necessary. Other Group Finder pages retain their original size. Geometry changes wait until combat ends, and unavailable or protected UI keeps the game window as a fallback. Exact font metrics, taint and live search behavior still require client testing.

## Языки интерфейса

Интерфейс следует языку клиента: enUS/enGB, ruRU, deDE, frFR, esES/esMX, zhCN, zhTW, koKR, ptBR и itIT. Для неизвестной локали остаётся английский резерв. Available search languages come from the game. Selecting them does not translate listings or change the language of your advertisements.
